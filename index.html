Privacy policy

Clear by design.

This policy explains how Jackpot Inc handles information when you use the iOS app or an official browser edition.

Effective August 7, 2026

01

Information we collect

Jackpot Inc does not ask for your email address, contacts, photos, precise location, payment information, or a separate username and password. If you enable Game Center, Apple supplies a scoped player identifier and display name. Jackpot Inc links those values to a random installation ID and live-ranking state. Apple processes purchase transactions; Jackpot Inc never receives your card or banking details. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver non-personalized ads, perform frequency capping and aggregated reporting, prevent fraud, and comply with law.

02

Ads and optional purchases

Jackpot Inc uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent tools. A banner appears at the bottom of the app, and a full-screen ad may appear at a natural break after fifteen minutes of active play. Jackpot Inc requests non-personalized treatment for every ad and does not ask for permission to track activity across other companies' apps and websites. Optional consumable play-coin packs use Apple's StoreKit purchase system. Apple provides verified transaction information so the app can deliver purchased coins and avoid delivering the same transaction twice.

03

Local play and live rankings

Game outcomes and recent-round history stay on your device. Your balance, favorites, daily rewards, collection, card-income ledger, creator-code status, equipped booster, biggest win, and cumulative losses are also saved locally. When Game Center and live rankings are enabled, Jackpot Inc receives your scoped Game Center identifier and display name, random installation ID, current play-coin balance, owned and sold card IDs, creator-code redemption status, equipped booster, referral share code, referrer and redemption status, one-time reward ledger, and server checkpoint times. The service uses that state to project card income for up to seven days, refresh the Most Money ranks once per minute while devices are offline, record creator-code redemptions reported by Game Center-connected players, prevent duplicate referral rewards, show how many successful invites used your personal referral code, and award each eligible player one 500,000-play-coin referral bonus. It also displays aggregate redemption totals reported by Game Center-connected players for each promotional creator code; those totals do not expose player identities. This information is used for app functionality and not for advertising or tracking.

04

Game Center

Apple manages Game Center authentication. Current balance, biggest win, and total losses may be submitted to Apple as leaderboard snapshots. Hidden achievements record cards you have ever collected so Apple can calculate worldwide ownership percentages. Your Game Center display name and live Most Money score are visible to other ranked players.

05

Advertising choices

Where required, the app asks for advertising consent before requesting ads. Settings includes Privacy Choices whenever Google's consent system requires it, allowing you to review or withdraw that consent. Jackpot Inc still requests non-personalized ad treatment after consent. Your device settings may also limit advertising identifiers. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy. Apple explains how it handles purchase information in its Privacy Policy. To report inappropriate or age-inappropriate advertising, email brycebehncke@icloud.com with a screenshot and the approximate time the ad appeared.

06

Retention and deletion

Live-ranking data is retained while needed to provide the leaderboard. Idempotency records used to prevent duplicate balance and referral updates expire after 30 days. Creator-code and referral redemption markers may be retained to prevent repeat rewards and inaccurate usage counts. You can request deletion of your Jackpot Inc live-ranking and referral record by emailing App Support. Deleting it does not delete information that Apple or Google independently controls under their own policies.

07

Browser editions and game portals

Official browser editions save game progress in your browser's local storage. They do not contain Apple StoreKit, Apple Game Center, or Google AdMob code, and they do not sell play coins. A browser game portal may provide its own hosting, account, cloud-save, leaderboard, or advertising service. When one of those services is enabled, that portal may process information such as your IP address, browser or device information, portal account identifier, saved-game data, ad interactions, and diagnostics under its own privacy policy. Jackpot Inc sends data to a portal only for features included in that portal's approved build. Clearing browser storage may erase progress that has not been saved by a portal.

08

Policy updates

If the app's privacy practices change, this page will be updated to describe the new practices and show a new effective date.

Play-coin notice

Just a game. Never real money.

Jackpot Inc uses fictional play coins that can be earned free. The iOS app may offer optional consumable play-coin purchases through Apple; official browser editions do not sell play coins. Play coins have no cash value, provide no prizes, and cannot be sold, transferred, redeemed, or exchanged for anything of real-world value.
