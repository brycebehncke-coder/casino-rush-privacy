Privacy policy

Clear by design.

This policy explains how Casino Rush handles information when you use the iOS app.

Effective July 29, 2026

01

Information we collect

Casino Rush does not ask for your email address, contacts, photos, precise location, payment information, or create its own player account. If you use Apple Game Center, Apple supplies your Game Center display name and manages your leaderboard identity. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud, and comply with law.

02

How the app works

Casino Rush uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent tools. A banner appears at the bottom of the app, and a full-screen ad may appear at a natural break after ten minutes of active play. Advertising requests are separate from game outcomes and play-coin activity.

03

Local gameplay state

Game outcomes and recent-play history stay locally on your device. Your current play-coin balance, favorites, largest single win, and cumulative losses are saved on your device. Casino Rush does not send gameplay activity to its own servers.

04

Advertising choices

Where required, the app asks for advertising consent before requesting ads and provides a way to reopen privacy choices. Your device settings may also limit advertising identifiers. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

05

Apple Game Center leaderboards

If you enable Game Center, Casino Rush submits three score totals to Apple: your current play-coin balance, largest profit from one round, and cumulative play coins lost. Apple handles those scores and your Game Center profile under the Apple Privacy Policy.

06

Policy updates

If the app's privacy practices change, this page will be updated to describe the new practices and show a new effective date.
